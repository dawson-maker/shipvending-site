What We Run

Three lines, one route.

Same self-serve model, same restocking discipline — three very different aisles depending on what the venue and its crowd actually want.

Live at Kirby Ice House

Flagship nightlife kiosk — tap-to-pay, always stocked, part of the room

LINE 01 — FLAGSHIP

Nightlife Vending

Our original line and still the majority of the fleet: multi-product kiosks carrying vapes, disposables, and nicotine pouches, parked inside the bars and lounges where the crowd already is.

Outdoor patio install

Dedicated pouch kiosk, wall-mounted for an always-open patio

LINE 02 — POUCH ONLY

Pouch Vending

Single-category kiosks that carry nicotine pouches and nothing else, for venues that want a fast, tobacco-free, no-smoke-or-spit option and nothing more cluttering the screen. Same tap-to-pay checkout and restocking schedule as every other machine we run.

Mystery pack kiosk (bar wall) · trading card kiosks (retail aisle & checkout)

LINE 03 — NEWEST

Trading Card & Mystery Pack Vending

Our newest line: Pokémon and other trading card packs, plus blind "mystery packs," sold out of retail-ready kiosks in liquor stores, convenience stores, and bars. Same self-serve model, a completely different aisle — and one of our fastest-growing categories.

The Hardware

Built like signage. Run like retail.

Every unit is a lit, branded fixture first — and a fully managed retail point second, whichever of the three lines it's running. Nothing about it feels like an afterthought bolted to the wall.

01

Tap-to-pay checkout

Contactless card and mobile payment, no cash box, no attendant. A purchase takes the same three seconds as a bar tab swipe.

02

Curated product mix

Each machine's lineup is set per venue — we track what actually sells and rotate slow movers out rather than letting a machine go stale.

03

Monitored, not guessed at

We check stock levels on a standing schedule and restock proactively, so a venue never has to flag a dead machine to us — we already know.
